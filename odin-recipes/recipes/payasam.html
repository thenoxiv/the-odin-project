CARAMAL PAYASAM 💗

Ingredients

  • Milk -500m
  • Vermicelli- 50g
  • Tapioca pearls - 30 g
  • Sugar -400g
  • Elaichi- 3 nos
  • Water - 1 litre
  • Cashew - 20nos
  • Raisins - 20nos
  • Ghee - 2 teaspoons

(Note : This recipe is passed down directly from the private collection of a certified culinary artist )

Recipe

The process ✨

  1. Have 1litre of water and tapioca pearls ,boil it till the core of the tapioca pearls are transparent.

  2. Add vermicelli and cook till it gets soft.

  3. In another vessel boil the milk and make it boil and add the elaichi.

  4. Take another pan , add the 400g of sugar and caramelise it without adding any water, once it has got its beautiful brown colour off the stove and cool it.
    (Don't worry of the CARAMAL getting hard ).

  5. Now add the boiled milk to the harend caramal and mix it well till the CARAMAL gets well into the milk.

  6. Now add the caramal milk to the boiled mixture of tapioca pearls and vermicelli.

  7. Now pour the ghee into a pan and add cashews and raisins, fry it till it gets light brown and add this to the payasam.

  8. Now our simple and different caramal payasam is ready to Serve Hot.
